💡 Tax Planning Notes

  • Straight-line: Even annual deductions over 27.5 years (residential) or 39 years (commercial)
  • MACRS: Accelerated depreciation using 200% declining balance switching to straight line — larger deductions in early years
  • Land is non-depreciable — only the building value ($280,000) qualifies for depreciation
  • Cost segregation studies can reclassify portions of the building to 5/7/15-year categories, accelerating deductions significantly
  • Consult a CPA before filing — individual circumstances vary
