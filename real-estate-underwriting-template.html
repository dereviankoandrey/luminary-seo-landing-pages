🔍 Section 3: Due Diligence Checklist

Complete every item before committing capital. Skipping even one is how deals go sideways.

  • Zoning verification: Confirm current zoning allows intended use. Check for pending changes in the district.
  • Title search: No liens, encumbrances, or ownership disputes. Order a commitment 48 hours before closing.
  • Structural inspection: Licensed inspector, not the seller's contractor. Foundation, roof, electrical, plumbing.
  • Rental comparables (if applicable): 3+ active and recently leased units within 0.5 miles confirming rent assumptions.
  • Sales comparables: 3+ comparable sales within 0.5 miles, sold in last 90 days, adjusted for differences.
  • Environmental assessment: Phase I if multifamily or commercial. Phase II if any red flags identified.
  • Permit history: Check county records for open permits, code violations, or unpermitted work.
  • Insurance quotes: Get actual quotes before underwriting — insurance costs vary dramatically by location and property type.
