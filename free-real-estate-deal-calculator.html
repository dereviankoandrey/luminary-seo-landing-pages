Why Most Investors Skip This Step

They don't. The operators who consistently build wealth run every deal through the same underwriting framework — and reject more deals than they accept.

Most investors fall into one of three traps:

  • The Gut Feel Trap — "The numbers seem okay" isn't a strategy. Without deterministic thresholds, you rationalize through bad deals.

  • The Sunk Cost Trap — You've already spent weeks on due diligence, so you convince yourself the deal works to justify the time invested.
  • The FOMO Trap — "If I don't buy now, someone else will." This is how experienced operators lose money on deals they know better to pass on.

This calculator applies the same deterministic thresholds used by professional developers with 20+ years of portfolio management experience. The math doesn't care about your emotions or timeline pressure.
