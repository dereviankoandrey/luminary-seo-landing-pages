🔄 The BRRRR Method — Step by Step

  1. Buy a distressed property below market value. The key is buying at a discount — typically 65-75% of ARV minus rehab costs.
  2. Rehab the property to increase its value and make it rent-ready. Keep renovation costs as close to estimate as possible.
  3. Rent out the property to generate cash flow. You need a tenant paying market rent for the refinance appraiser to validate your ARV.
  4. Refinance with a DSCR or traditional rental loan based on the new appraised value. Pull out most or all of your original capital.
  5. Repeat with recycled capital. The goal is scaling from 1 property to 5, 10, 20+ without adding more personal capital each cycle.

⚠️ Where BRRRR Goes Wrong (And How Our Calculator Catches It)

The Refinance Trap: Most BRRRR failures happen at the refinance step. The appraised value comes in below expectations, the lender's DSCR requirement is higher than anticipated, or rehab costs balloon. Our calculator shows you exactly what happens under conservative (75% LTV) vs standard (80%) vs aggressive (85%) lending scenarios so you can plan accordingly.
The Cash Flow Illusion: Even when your refinance pulls out all capital, the monthly mortgage payment on a DSCR loan is typically 1-2% higher than owner-occupied rates. Many BRRRR deals show positive cash flow at purchase but go negative after refinancing. Our calculator factors in the actual post-refi payment so you see real numbers.

📈 When BRRRR Makes Sense (And When It Doesn't)

BRRRR is most powerful in markets where:

  • ✅ You can buy properties at significant discounts (distressed sales, off-market deals)
  • ✅ Rehab costs are predictable and controllable (cosmetic updates vs structural work)
  • ✅ Rental demand supports the projected rent (job growth, population inflow)
  • ✅ DSCR lenders offer competitive rates in your market

BRRRR is risky when:

  • ❌ You're buying at full market value (no discount = no margin)
  • ❌ Rehab requires permits, structural work, or environmental remediation
  • ❌ The local rental market is saturated or declining
  • ❌ You need the property to cash flow positively from day one (refinance timing risk)
